# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Secondary and tertiary students exploring study and career directions on a phone or classroom desktop. They need a friendly, low-pressure way to recognize their interests without career-assessment jargon.

## Product Purpose

Career Ready City turns a Holland Code assessment into a six-stop city tour. Success means students finish the journey, understand their top three RIASEC themes, and leave with concrete career ideas worth exploring.

## Brand Personality

Playful, encouraging, energetic. The guide should feel like a supportive mentor and the city like a bold illustrated game, never a clinical assessment.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, childish gamification, casino-like reward loops, muted corporate career portals, and visual changes that replace the supplied team artwork.

## Design Principles

- Make progress visible and completion feel earned.
- Ask simple human questions before introducing career terminology.
- Preserve the illustrated city identity across every interaction.
- Turn results into useful next steps, not just a score.
- Keep the journey comfortable on phone and classroom desktop.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Do not communicate selection by color alone, retain keyboard focus, provide reduced-motion behavior, and use clear language suitable for students with varied English fluency.
