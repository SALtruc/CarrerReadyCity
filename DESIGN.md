# Career Ready City Design System

## Visual theme

Bold illustrated 2D city game derived from the supplied Figma exports: cobalt graph-paper world, cyan onboarding, black comic outlines, hard offset shadows, circular stamps, and editorial white panels.

## Color palette

- World blue: `#2458d3`
- Onboarding cyan: `#43ceda`
- Action yellow: `#ffcb05`
- Signal red: `#ed1b2f`
- Deep navy: `#09045b`
- Community pink: `#ef63dd`
- Ink: `#050505`
- Surface: `#ffffff`

## Typography

DM Sans carries interface and body copy. Archivo Black is reserved for illustrated place names and major result codes.

## Components

Controls use 2–3px black outlines, rounded containers, and hard black offset shadows. Selected answers combine yellow fill, a checkmark, `aria-pressed`, and a pressed shadow state.

## Layout

Mobile follows the vertical Figma tour. Desktop becomes a centered two-column city route with a maximum 1300px game canvas.

## Motion

Motion communicates navigation, selection, completion, and loading. Interaction feedback stays under 200ms. Effects collapse under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
